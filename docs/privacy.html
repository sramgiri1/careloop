CareLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-26

Information We Collect

CareLoop collects account details, task content, circle membership data, reminder and digest settings, and app usage information needed to run coordination features.

How Data Is Used

We use collected information to support care coordination, task assignment, reminders, daily digests, and basic service operations.

Third-Party Services

CareLoop uses third-party services including Resend for email delivery, Apple APNs for push notifications, and Railway or similar hosting for backend infrastructure.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as needed to operate the service and support users. Users can request deletion of their account and related data.

No Clinic Integration

CareLoop does not integrate with clinics, hospitals, healthcare providers, or electronic medical record systems. Clinic integration is permanently off.

FTC Health Breach Notification Rule

If a qualifying breach affects personal health information handled by CareLoop, we will follow the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and applicable notice requirements.
